(2)  MANIFESTO

We don't sell clothes. We sell the confidence of a 47x markdown.

Founder Fits exists because somewhere between the pitch deck and the doomsday clock of runway, a generation of operators realized the fastest path to conviction is a really good collar. We build for that operator. We build for the "energy," the "community," the "we." We build costumes for people who will absolutely say "elevate the global consciousness" in a board meeting and mean it.

This is not a fund. We do not deploy capital. We deploy fabric, at scale, into a market that has never needed it more: the market of pretending, convincingly, until the Series B.
