(2)  MANIFESTO

We don't sell clothes. We sell the confidence of a 47x markdown.

Every category needs a founder willing to wear the costume before they've earned the role. We believe the outfit comes first — the cap table, if we're being honest, is a lagging indicator.

FounderFits exists because somewhere between the pitch deck and the doomsday clock of runway, a generation of operators realized the fastest path to conviction is a really good collar. We build for that operator. We build for the "energy," the "community," the "we." We build for people who will absolutely say "elevate the global consciousness" in a board meeting and mean it.

This is not a fund. We do not deploy capital. We deploy costumes, at scale, into a market that has never needed it more: the market of pretending, convincingly, until the Series B.

Frequently asked questions

What is FounderFits?

A parody costume and accessories store. The brand is a joke about startup culture; the products and the orders are real.

What are the products?

Two products. The Barefoot Tech CEO Costume shoe covers: a free-size pair of oversized vinyl covers shaped like bare feet, $24.99 USD, worn over your shoes (shoes and outfit not included). And the Stealth Mode Founder Costume turtleneck: a black long-sleeve turtleneck in sizes S to 2XL, $34.99 USD.

What is a funny, easy Halloween costume idea?

Two ideas from us. The shoe covers ($24.99) go over the shoes you're already wearing, no assembly. The turtleneck ($34.99) is the "founder uniform": black, long sleeve, jeans not included. Both ship to the US only, with an estimated delivery of 5–11 business days, so order early. Shoe covers → Turtleneck →

The shoe covers are sold as one free size, one pair, and we don't publish exact dimensions or a weight. The turtleneck is slim fit in S, M, L, XL and 2XL. Asian sizing can run 1–2 sizes smaller than US sizes, so if you're between sizes, take the larger one.

What is the turtleneck made of?

Knit fabric, polyester and wool.

Where do you ship and how long does it take?

We currently ship to addresses in the United States only. Estimated delivery time is 5–11 business days. See the shipping policy.

What is the return policy?

30 days from delivery. Items must be unused, unworn and in original packaging, and worn costume items can't be returned. If you change your mind, you pay the return shipping. See the refund policy.

Is this a joke?

The brand is parody. The products and your order are real.

No. FounderFits sells a pair of vinyl shoe covers, a black turtleneck and several bad jokes.

Is this affiliated with Founders Fund, WeWork, or Adam Neumann?

No. This site is a parody / satire built for fun. It is not endorsed by, sponsored by, or affiliated with any real fund, company, or individual referenced or implied here.
